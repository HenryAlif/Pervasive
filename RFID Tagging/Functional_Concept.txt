1. TAGGING TECHNOLOGY USED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Motion employs TWO distinct tagging technologies, each optimized for its specific
asset type and operational pattern:

RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) — Passive Tags:
  • Used for: Production Jigs (Station 1–12 Assembly Line) & Transport Pallets
    (Conveyor to Warehouse)
  • Tag Type: Passive RFID tags (no battery; powered by reader's RF field)
  • Read Method: AUTOMATIC, via fixed RFID readers placed at strategic points
    (assembly line stations, conveyor entry/exit)
  • Trigger: No human action required. Tags are read automatically when the
    asset passes within reader range
  • Frequency: Typically 860–960 MHz (UHF band for longer read range ~4–5 meters)
  • Why this choice: Jigs and pallets are recurring assets that follow fixed
    paths. Automatic detection requires zero operator interaction, reducing error
    and enabling seamless, real-time tracking as production flows

Barcode (1D/2D Linear/QR Codes) — Passive Labels:
  • Used for: Finished Seat Goods (completed products)
  • Read Method: MANUAL, via handheld gun scanner (held by operator, pressed to
    label to trigger laser/imager read)
  • Trigger: Operator must deliberately scan each seat's barcode
  • Format: 1D barcode or 2D QR code printed on each finished seat's shipping label
  • Why this choice: Finished seats are one-time products flowing outbound once.
    Unlike reusable jigs, they don't need automated ambient detection. Barcode is
    ideal for point-of-scan traceability: operator confirms "this seat, at this
    moment, has this product code, lot number, date"—full provenance in one scan

Key Insight: Two technologies serve the two-tier system:
  • RFID = Passive, continuous monitoring of reusable production equipment
    (pervasive, background sensing)
  • Barcode = Active, on-demand traceability of products (explicit act of
    confirmation)


2. DATA FLOW DIAGRAM (System Flow)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

TWO PARALLEL DATA PATHS:

PATH A: RFID (Automatic, Production Equipment)
─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Physical Flow:
    Jig/Pallet moves through Assembly Line (Station 1 → 12) or on Conveyor
    system toward Warehouse

  Data Flow:
    [Jig/Pallet + RFID Tag]
         ↓ (passes reader range automatically)
    [Fixed RFID Reader] — detects tag ID, reads sensor data, records timestamp
         ↓ (connects via Ethernet or wireless)
    [PC/Industrial Computer] — validates data, formats payload
         ↓ (stores or streams)
    [Database (PC Local or Remote)] — stores Jig/Pallet movement records
         ↓ (query on demand or stream to app)
    [Mobile App (Dashboard, Alerts, Analytics)]
         ↓ (user views: asset status, location, maintenance due)
    [4 Teams: MES, Maintenance, PPIC, PD]

  Payload per Read (stored in Database):
    - RFID Jig ID (e.g., JIG-0847)
    - Station Number (e.g., 05) OR Conveyor Segment
    - Sequence Number (production batch identifier)
    - Datetime (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS)

  Frequency: Continuous, automated — every jig/pallet triggers a read as it
  passes each reader (5–20 reads per jig per production cycle, depending on
  number of readers)

  Key Property: NO MANUAL ACTION. Data flows push-based (reader initiates) and
  automatic (operator unaware).


PATH B: Barcode (Manual, Finished Goods)
──────────────────────────────────────────
  Physical Flow:
    Finished Seat passes through shipping/QC station. Operator holds Seat +
    Gun Scanner.

  Data Flow:
    [Finished Seat + Barcode Label]
         ↓ (operator points gun scanner, pulls trigger)
    [Handheld Gun Scanner] — optical read, decodes barcode payload
         ↓ (via USB or wireless to PC)
    [PC/Warehouse Terminal] — receives barcode data, validates, formats
         ↓ (stores)
    [Database]
         ↓ (query on demand or synced to app)
    [Mobile App (Barcode Scan Screen, read-only tracking)]
         ↓ (user views: sequence, product code, part number, lot, datetime)
    [4 Teams — primarily PPIC & PD for traceability/analytics]

  Payload per Scan (stored in Database):
    - Sequence Number (unique production sequence)
    - Product Code (e.g., SEAT-XYZ-2026-10-22)
    - Part Number (component ID)
    - Lot Number (batch/shift identifier)
    - Datetime (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS of scan)

  Frequency: Event-driven — exactly once per seat (or once per scan if re-scanned)

  Key Property: MANUAL ACTION REQUIRED. Data flows pull-based (operator initiates
  scan) and explicit (operator confirms "I scanned this specific seat").


CRITICAL DIFFERENCE:
  RFID: Background, passive, continuous, ambient awareness — system watches
        without anyone's deliberate act
  Barcode: Foreground, active, event-driven, explicit confirmation — operator
           must choose to capture data


3. USER ROLES AND INTERACTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Four Internal Teams Access Motion:

MES TEAM (Manufacturing Execution System)
  Role: Monitor real-time production flow across Assembly Line Stations 1–12
        and conveyor to warehouse
  Primary Need: "Where are jigs/pallets right now? Are they moving as scheduled?"
  Interactions:
    • View Dashboard: see active jigs at each station, pallets in transit
    • Monitor Alerts: catch jigs stuck at a station (exceeded time threshold) or
      RFID reader failures (offline status)
    • Analytics: analyze bottlenecks (e.g., which station delays most often)

Maintenance Team
  Role: Proactive & reactive maintenance on jigs and pallets
  Primary Need: "Which jigs/pallets are due for maintenance? Which have failed
                recently?"
  Interactions:
    • View Alerts: maintenance-due alerts (e.g., "JIG-0847 overdue for
      lubrication")
    • View Dashboard: jigs tagged with "Attention Required" or "Offline" status
    • Manage RFID: register newly serviced jigs, update tag associations
    • Analytics: identify which jigs/pallets fail most frequently (leading
      candidates for redesign or upgrade)

PPIC TEAM (Production Planning and Inventory Control)
  Role: Plan production schedules, track seat goods, control inventory
  Primary Need: "Can I trace this finished seat? What batch is it from? Where
                will it go next?"
  Interactions:
    • Barcode Scan Screen: scan finished seats to capture sequence, product code,
      lot, datetime for shipping/inventory
    • Dashboard: overview of production rate (jigs moving through stations)
    • Analytics: historical trace of how many seats completed per hour/day/week

PD TEAM (Product Development, Support)
  Role: Support operations, analyze trends, optimize system
  Primary Need: "What's the big picture? How efficient is this line? What can we
                improve?"
  Interactions:
    • Dashboard: high-level view of system health (total jigs, in-maintenance,
      offline)
    • Analytics: detailed trend analysis—alert frequency, mean-time-between-failure
      (MTBF), production throughput
    • Alerts: escalation/summary view of critical issues

ACCESS MODEL: NO ROLE-BASED HIDING
  All four teams see all seven screens (Splash, Login, Dashboard, Barcode Scan,
  Manage RFID, Alerts, Analytics). The system does NOT hide Alerts from MES or
  Barcode Scan from Maintenance. Instead, each team focuses on the data most
  relevant to them, but the option to explore other screens is always available.
  Login differentiates user identity only for audit trail and notification routing,
  not for permission boundaries.

COMMON INTERACTION FLOW:
  1. Open Motion app on mobile (or tablet)
  2. Login with User ID (e.g., MES-001, MAINT-05, PPIC-team, PD-support)
  3. Land on Dashboard → rapid health check
  4. If issues spotted → drill into Alerts, Detail view, or Manage RFID
  5. If tracking needed → Barcode Scan or Analytics


4. PERVASIVE COMPUTING ELEMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Motion embodies pervasive computing (ubiquitous computing) through AMBIENT SENSING
and CONTEXT-AWARE OPERATIONS:

Ambient Sensing (RFID Passive Detection):
  • Jigs moving through assembly line → RFID readers continuously monitor without
    operator awareness
  • Data collection happens in the background: no app opened, no button pressed,
    no conscious user action
  • The system "watches the environment" — location tracking emerges from
    infrastructure, not from explicit data entry
  • This is the essence of "pervasive": computing is embedded into the physical
    production environment, not confined to a device

Context-Awareness:
  • System always knows: What jig is where, when did it arrive, what's the next
    station
  • Users don't query "where is JIG-0847?" — the system already knows and
    proactively alerts if it's overdue (e.g., "JIG-0847 stuck at Station 07 for
    15 min")
  • Alerts are triggered by implicit rules (timeout thresholds, movement anomalies)
    without explicit user search

Mobile App as an Interface to Embedded Sensing:
  • The core computation (tracking, alerting, trend analysis) happens via RFID
    infrastructure in the production line
  • The mobile app is NOT the source of data — it's a window into that embedded
    computation
  • Users access the app from anywhere in the plant (assembly line, warehouse,
    office) and get immediate view of real-time state

Why This Matters (Pervasive Computing):
  Traditional systems: Worker carries clipboard, writes down jig ID and time
                       → manual, error-prone, time-delayed
  Motion (Pervasive): RFID reader detects jig automatically → system records
                      → app notifies → worker responds
  The computing "disappears" into the environment; users interact with results,
  not mechanics.

Ubiquitous Identification (Tagging):
  Each jig/pallet has a persistent RFID identity. That identity is recognized
  automatically wherever a reader is installed. Crucially, this is NOT limited
  to "during production" — if a jig moves to a maintenance bay with a reader,
  it's still recognized. This enables the "seamless tracking across locations"
  that defines pervasive systems.


5. INTEGRATION POSSIBILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Motion is architected to integrate with adjacent systems and future extensions:

MES/ERP Integration:
  • Motion can sync jig/pallet status to existing Manufacturing Execution System
    (MES) or Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)
  • Example: When a jig completes Station 12, Motion auto-sends completion event
    to ERP → ERP schedules next production task
  • Benefit: Eliminates double-entry; real-time production visibility in corporate
    reporting

IoT Sensor Integration:
  • Future: Attach temperature/vibration/pressure sensors to jigs
  • RFID readers could also collect sensor telemetry alongside tag ID
  • Enables predictive maintenance: "Jig-0847 vibration trending upward →
    schedule bearing replacement before failure"
  • Example: integrate vibration data → ML model predicts MTBF → trigger
    maintenance alert

Cloud Backend & Multi-Location Access:
  • Current: RFID readers → Local PC → Local database → Mobile app (on same
    local network)
  • Future: Cloud sync (Firebase, AWS, Azure) → all plants worldwide see unified
    dashboard
  • Enables central Maintenance team (HQ) to support multiple manufacturing sites

Barcode Integration with Existing Production Lines:
  • Many seat factories already have barcode printers, gun scanners, and barcode
    databases
  • Motion can plug into that existing barcode workflow: gun scanner sends data to
    Motion database, app retrieves tracing info
  • Benefit: No need for separate barcode infrastructure — motion leverages what's
    already there

Supply Chain Tracking (Future):
  • Extend barcode tracking beyond finished goods: track pallets of seats through
    warehouse, shipping, delivery
  • Integrate with shipping partners' systems: "This pallet (from Motion barcode)
    is en route to customer X"
  • Full traceability: raw materials in → production → finished goods → customer
    site

Predictive Maintenance via Analytics:
  • Motion collects jig/pallet history (uptime, failures, maintenance events)
  • Integrate with analytics platform (Tableau, Power BI, or custom ML) → trend
    forecasting
  • Use case: "In the past 60 days, JIG-xxx fails every 9 days. Recommend
    preventive replacement on Day 8"

PART 2: SCREEN PURPOSE & FUNCTION (7 Screens)

S-01: SPLASH SCREEN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Establish brand presence and load-time experience. First impression of Motion
  app — builds confidence that user launched the correct, official application
  (not a phishing or test app).

Function:
  • Displays Motion logo, app name, optional tagline ("Smart Asset Tracking")
  • Shows a loading animation or progress indicator (app initializing database
    connection, checking RFID reader status, verifying token)
  • Auto-advances to Login after 2–3 seconds on first launch; skips on subsequent
    launches (token cached)
  • Sets the visual tone: industrial, professional, efficient (navy-and-gold
    brand palette signals industrial control room, not consumer app)


S-02: LOGIN SCREEN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Authenticate users before system access. Ensures only authorized 4-team members
  (MES, Maintenance, PPIC, PD) can enter. Differentiates user identity for audit
  logging and notification routing.

Function:
  • Collects User ID (e.g., MES-001, MAINT-05) + Password
  • Validates credentials against local database or corporate directory
  • Issues JWT token valid for session (stored in localStorage)
  • Optional "Remember Me" for shared tablets (avoid re-entry for frequent users)
  • Sets identity context: subsequent screens know "this is Maintenance Team" or
    "this is PPIC" (used for audit trail, Slack notifications, report exports)
  • Bridge between physical plant (where user works) and digital system (app on
    phone)


S-03: DASHBOARD (Main Screen)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Central entry point — answer the core question "Is production normal right now?"
  in under 3 seconds. This is where all 4 teams START every session. MES wants
  flow status, Maintenance wants equipment health, PPIC wants throughput, PD wants
  system overview — all land here first.

Function:
  • Shows 4-tile KPI summary: Total Jigs, Total Pallets, Jigs in Maintenance,
    Pallets in Maintenance (immediately tells operator: "Everything nominal" or
    "Alert: 2 jigs overdue")
  • Lists all jigs/pallets as Status Cards, auto-sorted by urgency (Offline first,
    then Attention Required, then Active) — urgent items always top-left (where
    eye naturally falls on mobile screen)
  • Each card shows asset ID, current station/location, status badge (color:
    green/amber/red), sequence, last-detected time
  • Optional "Recent Alerts" panel summarizing last 3–5 unresolved issues
  • Bottom navigation tabs: Dashboard (active) · Barcode Scan · Manage RFID ·
    Alerts · Analytics
  • Landing point for all flow: from here, user drills into detail (tap card → see
    history), scans barcode (tap nav → S-04), checks alerts (tap nav → S-06), etc.


S-04: BARCODE SCAN / TRACKING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Bridge between production line and outbound/inventory: track finished seats
  through barcode. Operator scans seat barcode (or manually searches by sequence)
  to confirm: "This seat, this lot, this product code, at this time." Primary
  users: PPIC (tracking) and PD (analytics seed data).

Function:
  • Provides barcode scan interface: camera input (if device supports) or manual
    barcode/sequence entry
  • Once scanned: displays barcode info card (sequence, product code, part number,
    lot number, datetime)
  • Read-only display: app is NOT a barcode input tool (that's the gun scanner on
    line) — it's a lookup/verification tool
  • Optional: show full barcode-scan history from this session (previous seats
    scanned this shift)
  • Use case flow: PPIC operator completes production run → uses Motion barcode
    screen to cross-check 3–5 random seats' barcodes → confirms batch integrity
    → approves shipment


S-05: MANAGE RFID
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Represent the bridge between physical RFID tags and digital identities. This is
  where system acknowledges: "We have a jig with this RFID ID; we're calling it
  JIG-0847; here's its asset info." Enables creation, updating, and deletion of
  tag-to-asset mappings.

Function:
  • Lists all registered RFID tags (both jigs and pallets): RFID ID, Asset Type
    (Jig/Pallet), Custom Name (optional), Current Status badge
  • "Add Tag" button → form to register new RFID (when new jig/pallet acquired):
    enter RFID ID, select type, assign name
  • Edit tag → update asset info (name, type, or retire tag)
  • Delete tag → deregister RFID (when asset scrapped or transferred out)
  • Filter tabs: All · Jigs · Pallets (quick scan for specific asset type)
  • Action: manages the "registry" — ensures every RFID tag detected by readers
    has a corresponding database record, so alerts/dashboards have human-readable
    asset names


S-06: ALERTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Escalation channel — surface all unresolved issues (production delays,
  maintenance overdue, RFID offline, etc.) in one place. Users don't have to
  hunt for problems; Alerts brings them to the foreground.

Function:
  • Scrollable list of active alerts, sorted by severity (Offline red first, then
    Attention Required amber)
  • Each alert shows: icon (color-coded) · title (e.g., "JIG-0847 Overdue for
    Maintenance") · timestamp (when alert fired) · action button (Dismiss, View
    Detail, Acknowledge)
  • Filter tabs: All · Attention Required · Offline (quick filter for severity
    level)
  • Empty state: "No Alerts — All systems running normally" (confidence signal)
  • Use case: Maintenance Team opens Alerts → sees "PLT-456 RFID Offline for 30
    min" → investigates whether reader failed or pallet was removed from conveyor
    → resolves


S-07: ANALYTICS (Optional but Recommended)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Purpose:
  Aggregate historical patterns for optimization. PD team + leadership use this to
  answer: "What's our reliability? What jigs/pallets fail most? What's the
  bottleneck?" Long-term planning and system improvement.

Function:
  • Main visualization: bar or line chart showing alert frequency over time (last
    7 days, 30 days, 90 days — date range picker)
  • Breakdown by alert type: Offline alerts vs. Attention Required (shows severity
    distribution)
  • Optional breakdown by asset type: Jigs vs. Pallets (reveals which asset class
    is more troublesome)
  • Summary KPIs: Total Alerts (period), Average Alerts per Day, Offline Time
    (hours)
  • Optional: detailed data table (Period · Alert Count · MTBF · Avg Resolution
    Time) — exportable for reports
  • Use case: PD reviews Analytics weekly → spots that Jig-xxx fails every 9 days
    → recommends predictive maintenance or upgrade

CONCLUSION
================================================================================

Motion is a PURPOSE-BUILT, PERVASIVE-COMPUTING SYSTEM that makes production flow
transparent. RFID ambient sensing eliminates manual tracking; the mobile app brings
that data to every team member's phone.

The 7 screens work as a cohesive flow:
  1. Splash + Login → authenticate user
  2. Dashboard → rapid health check (the starting point for every session)
  3. From Dashboard: branch to Barcode Scan (track outbound), Manage RFID
     (registry), Alerts (escalation), or Analytics (trends)
  4. Each screen serves a specific decision point in the operator's workflow

By making the invisible (RFID data) visible (mobile app), Motion enables
predictive decisions (Maintenance teams prevent failures, PPIC plans with
confidence, PD optimizes operations).
